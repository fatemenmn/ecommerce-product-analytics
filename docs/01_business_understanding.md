## Key Business & Product Questions

1. **User Retention & Repurchase:** What percentage of unique users (`customer_unique_id`) make a second purchase on Olist, and what is the average time window between the first and second purchase?

2. **Delivery Performance vs. Customer Satisfaction:** How strongly does delivery delay (actual delivery date exceeding estimated delivery date) correlate with low review scores (1 or 2 stars)?

3. **Fulfillment Lead Time:** What is the average fulfillment lead time from purchase timestamp to actual delivery, and how does it vary across different Brazilian states?

4. **Product Category Performance:** Which product categories generate the highest Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) vs. which categories have the highest order cancellation rates?

5. **Payment Preference & Average Order Value (AOV):** Does offering multi-installment payment options (`payment_installments > 1`) lead to a significantly higher Average Order Value compared to single-payment orders?

6. **Seller Concentration Risk:** What proportion of total marketplace sales (GMV) is generated by the top 5% of sellers, and does seller concentration pose a risk to catalog diversity?

7. **Order Status Funnel Drop-off:** At what stage of the order lifecycle (`created` -> `approved` -> `shipped` -> `delivered`) do most order failures/cancellations occur?

8. **Geographic Logistics Bottlenecks:** Which buyer-seller state pairs exhibit the highest delivery latency, and how does distance impact shipping cost (`freight_value`)?

9. **Impact of Freight Costs on Conversion/AOV:** What is the average freight-to-price ratio per order, and does higher relative shipping cost correlate with higher review churn?

10. **Customer Satisfaction Drivers:** What are the main quantifiable factors (e.g., delivery speed, freight price, seller rating) that differentiate 5-star reviewed orders from 1-star reviewed orders?
